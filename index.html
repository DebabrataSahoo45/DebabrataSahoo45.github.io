RESEARCH PROJECT 01

Impact of Social Isolation on Depression Among Older Adults of India

A statistical study using LASI Wave 1 and binary logistic regression.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

This study examines the association between social isolation and depression among older adults in India. The analysis uses data from the Longitudinal Ageing Study in India (LASI), Wave 1.

The study population consists of adults aged 60 years and above, with binary logistic regression used to examine the relationship between depression and different dimensions of social isolation.

30,741 RESPONDENTS
60+ AGE GROUP
LASI DATA SOURCE
4 REGRESSION MODELS

OBJECTIVES

  1. To examine the relationship between social isolation and depression among older adults in India.
  2. To assess gender differences in depression.
  3. To examine whether the effect of social isolation on depression varies by gender.

DATA & VARIABLES

Outcome

Depression

Social Isolation

Living arrangement, marital status, social organization membership, visits to relatives/friends and loneliness.

Socioeconomic Factors

Age, gender, education, wealth and place of residence.

Health Factors

Chronic disease and related health characteristics.

METHODOLOGY

01 Data Preparation
02 Descriptive Analysis
03 Bivariate Analysis
04 Logistic Regression

KEY FINDINGS

01

Loneliness showed a strong association with depression among older adults.

02

Living alone was associated with higher odds of depression.

03

Frequent social interaction was associated with lower odds of depression.

04

Gender, education and socioeconomic characteristics were also considered in the adjusted models.
